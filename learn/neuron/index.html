Neuron

BlackSystemCommonCard 33/70

The smallest processing unit in a neural network — many of them together form the whole model.

In real life

One artificial neuron just adds up some numbers and decides how strongly to pass a signal on. The power comes from millions working together.

Try it (2 minutes)

Be a neuron: a friend holds up fingers on each hand. Add them up, and if the total is more than 5, clap. That's one neuron's job.
