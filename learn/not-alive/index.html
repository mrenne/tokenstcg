Humans and AI

Not Alive

GreenSystemCommonCard 1/70

AI systems can perform human-like tasks but aren't alive or conscious.

In real life

A chatbot can write "I'm so happy to help!" but it doesn't feel happy. It picked words that usually come next in friendly messages.

Try it (2 minutes)

List three things you did today that need being alive: feeling hungry, being tired, missing someone. Could an AI really do any of them?
