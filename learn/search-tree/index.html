Search Tree

BlueSystemRareCard 22/70

A branching map of possible moves an AI explores to find the best one, like in tic-tac-toe.

In real life

Game-playing AIs look ahead at possible moves, like "if I go here, they'll go there," to pick the best one.

Try it (2 minutes)

Play tic-tac-toe and say your thinking out loud: "If I go here, they could…" You're searching a tree.

Talk about it

Why might exploring every possible move become impossible for a more complex game?

In the game
