So how does it work?

The percentages are just counts. After the word "the," this AI has seen some words more often than others, so it bets on those. That's it.

Real chatbots work the same basic way, just enormously bigger: they learned from billions of pages instead of a few stories. That makes their guesses much better. They still predict likely words; they don't understand them the way you do.

Switch to Space stories and start over. Same AI, different reading, completely different sentences. An AI can only reflect what it learned from, including the mistakes and unfair ideas in its data.
