AI Isn't Magic

Any colorActionChaseCard 8/70

AI isn't sentient or magical — it's a tool built by people.

In real life

When a chatbot answers a question, it isn't thinking it over. It's predicting likely words, very quickly, using patterns from text it read.

Try it (2 minutes)
