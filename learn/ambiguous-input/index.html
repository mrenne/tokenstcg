Ambiguous Input

BlueActionCommonCard 23/70

Poorly represented or unclear data makes it hard for an AI system to act reliably.

In real life

A voice assistant might mishear "write" as "right" when there's background noise.

Try it (2 minutes)

Say a sentence with a word that has two meanings, like "bat" or "bark." How did your listener know which one you meant?

Talk about it

Why might unclear instructions make an AI "freeze up" or give a bad answer?
