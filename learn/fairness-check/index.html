Fairness Check

WhiteToolCommonCard 48/70

Evaluating whether an AI system's decisions are fair to everyone affected.

In real life

Before using an AI to help choose who gets a loan, testers check whether it treats different groups of people equally.

Try it (2 minutes)

Is it fair if everyone gets the same thing, or if everyone gets what they need? Argue both sides for one minute each.

Talk about it

Whose idea of "fair" should count when people disagree?

In the game

Turn this card sideways to put 1 Flag on target System whose Capability is higher than its Trust. It costs 2 Data to play, and you can put it in your Training Set. How to play
