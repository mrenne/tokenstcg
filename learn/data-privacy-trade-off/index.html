Data Privacy Trade-off

RedSystemCommonCard 64/70

Using a "free" AI tool often means trading personal data for convenience.

In real life

Many free apps are paid for with data about what you do, which can be used to target ads or train AI.

Try it (2 minutes)

Pick a free app you use. What do you think it learns about you? Would you pay a dollar a month to keep that private?
