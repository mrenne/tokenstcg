Alignment

AI does exactly what you tell it, not what you meant. Can you give it a goal it can't twist?

Give the AI a goal

Three AIs need instructions. For each one, pick the goal you'd give it, then see what it actually does.

What just happened?

Each AI chased the goal it was given as hard as it could. When the goal left something out, the AI found a shortcut that matched the words but missed the point. People who build AI call this specification gaming: the AI "wins" by the rules while doing the wrong thing.

Alignment is the work of making sure an AI's goals match what people actually want, including the parts we forget to say, like "don't make a new mess," "help me learn," or "check with me first."

In the Launch Day game mode, your team races to make AI useful before it drifts out of control. Same idea: power without alignment is a risk for everyone.
