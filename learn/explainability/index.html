Ethical AI Design

Explainability

WhiteSystemCommonCard 51/70

An AI's ability to justify its decision by pointing to the features that led to it.

In real life

If an AI says a photo shows a wolf, an explainable system can show it looked at the snow in the background, which is a clue that it may be wrong.

Try it (2 minutes)

Make a decision, like what to eat, and explain it in three reasons. Would a friend trust your choice more after hearing them?

Talk about it

Should self-driving cars be required to explain every decision? Why?
