About

A project by Mike Renne (opens in a new tab)

Tokens TCG is a “print-and-play” physical trading card game for the classroom. Educators print out the cards and use them in their classrooms to teach about generative AI (and related technologies) without students having to use a device.

Extension activities can move the game into the digital world. Learn more on the Educator Resources page.
