The Human Trainer

GreenSystemCommonCard 2/70

Humans label the examples an AI learns from.

In real life

Before a photo app can find "dogs" in your pictures, people somewhere labeled thousands of photos as dog or not-dog.

Try it (2 minutes)

Sort the snacks in your lunch into "healthy" and "treat." Did a classmate sort them the same way? An AI learns whatever labels its trainers choose.

Talk about it

If a labeler makes mistakes, what does the model learn?

In the game
